### Public views (6)

`dir_businesses`, `dir_images`, `dir_products`, `dir_services`, `dir_promotions`, `dir_reviews`.

The public only reads these views. They include only approved, non-suspended businesses, and only the fields the business's package allows. A street address and map pin appear only if the owner ticked "show my address".

### Storage

* `business-media` (public): logos, photos and product pictures, stored in one folder per business. Only that business's managers and admins can write to it.
* `business-docs` (private): verification and claim documents. Admins open them through links that expire after 2 minutes.

### Security rules (enforced in the database, not just the pages)

* Owners can only change their own businesses. They cannot approve, verify or feature a listing, grant themselves a package, add other managers, or change payments.
* A paid package never gives a verified badge.
* Reviews:
  * need a signed-in account with a confirmed email
  * owners can't review their own business
  * every review is held for moderation
  * an edited review goes back to moderation
  * owners can reply but cannot hide or delete reviews
* Payments:
  * owners can only *report* an M-Pesa or bank reference; admins confirm it
  * prices are calculated on the server, including annual discounts and codes
* An expired package falls back to Free automatically, and nothing is deleted.
* Enquiries and reports are rate-limited for each connection.
* Views and contact taps are counted once per visitor per day. Owners' own visits aren't counted, and readers can switch counting off in Cookie Settings.
* Every change is written to `business_audit_logs`.

## Needs you

1. **Approve or complete Patricia Bakes.** It is a *draft* that contains only the name, category, description and product categories you gave. Add the logo and real contact details in the wizard, then approve it in the admin screen: **All businesses → Approve**.
2. **Payment details.** Enter your M-Pesa Paybill or Till and bank details in **Advertising admin → Settings**. Directory invoices show the same details. Until then, invoices only say IMMA Media will send payment details.
3. **After approving listings**, publish their Google-friendly pages:
   ```
   node tools/build_directory.js
   ```
   Then commit and push. Approved listings are visible straight away without this step, through `listing.html`.
4. **Legal review.** Please read the directory terms and the new Privacy Policy section. The retention periods are proposals: 30 days to delete a listing, 12 months for documents, 24 months for enquiries and statistics.
5. **Supabase sign-in redirect.** If sign-in returns you to the wrong page from the directory, add `https://immanews.com/**` under **Authentication → URL Configuration → Redirect URLs**.

## Not built yet (needs a provider or your decision)

* **Card payments and M-Pesa STK Push.** These need a Safaricom Daraja account or a card provider such as Pesapal, Flutterwave or Paystack. Their keys must go in a Supabase Edge Function, never in the website. Payments are checked by hand until then.
* **Email or SMS** renewal reminders and enquiry alerts. These need a messaging service.
* **Automatic page rebuilds.** A scheduled GitHub Action could run `build_directory.js` every few hours.
* **Full marketplace checkout.** The catalogue has prices, availability and product groups, ready for it later.
