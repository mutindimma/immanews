Questions

Frequently asked questions

When does my advert go live?

On the start date you choose, once our team has approved the advert and your payment has been confirmed. You can follow each step in your advertiser dashboard.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa or bank transfer. After approval you will see the payment details in your dashboard. Enter your M-Pesa confirmation code or bank reference and we will verify it. Card payments and automatic M-Pesa prompts (STK Push) are not available yet.

Will my advert be the only one in its space?

Not unless we agree that in writing. A small number of adverts rotate in each space so that every advertiser gets fair exposure. The booking form shows if a space is already full for your dates.

What reports will I get?

Your dashboard shows how many times your advert was seen and clicked, the click-through rate, and a desktop versus mobile split, by day. We filter out bots and repeated views. We do not estimate or inflate figures.

What is a sponsored article?

A story about your business, published in our news feed with a clear Sponsored Content label. You supply the text and image; our editors check it for accuracy and tone. It is kept separate from our independent journalism.

What adverts do you not accept?

We do not accept misleading health claims, get-rich-quick or pyramid schemes, illegal products or services, gambling without a valid licence, hateful or discriminatory content, or adverts that pretend to be news. Full details are in our advertising terms.

Can I change my advert after it is approved?

Contact us and we will help. To protect readers, every change is reviewed again before it appears.

Do you issue invoices and receipts?

Yes. An invoice is created when you submit a campaign and a receipt when payment is confirmed. You can print or save both as PDF from your dashboard.
