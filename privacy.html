This policy explains what personal data IMMA News collects when you use immanews.com, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have. We aim to follow Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who we are

IMMA News is a digital news and information platform owned and published by IMMA Media, based in Nairobi, Kenya. For the personal data described here, IMMA Media is the data controller. You can reach us using the details in Contact us.

2. What we collect

If you just read the site

You can read every story without an account. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics. Like any website, the services that deliver our pages (listed in section 5) receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type so they can send you the page.

Advert measurement. IMMA News shows adverts that businesses buy directly from us. To tell advertisers honestly how often their advert was seen and clicked, we record each view and click with: the advert, the date and time, whether you are on a phone or a computer, a random code created for your browser session (stored in your browser's session storage and deleted when you close the tab), and a one-way coded version of your IP address that changes every day. We cannot work out your IP address from it, and we do not link it to your name or account. We use these records only to count views and clicks and to filter out bots and fake clicks. Advertisers see totals only.

If you create an account (Sign in with Google)

  • From Google: your name, email address and profile picture. We never receive your Google password, and we never post anything on your behalf.
  • Your choices on our site: the stories you save, and whether you want email updates.
  • Account details: the date you joined and your account type (reader, or staff for our team).

If you advertise with us

  • Business and contact details: business name, contact person, email, phone, website, business category, billing name and address, KRA PIN (optional) and logo.
  • Campaign records: your bookings, artwork, sponsored article text, invoices, payment references (such as M-Pesa confirmation codes) and amounts, and the date you accepted our advertising terms.
  • Advertising enquiries: the name, business, phone or email and message you send through the enquiry form on our Advertise page.

We do not collect card or bank account numbers. Payments are made by you through M-Pesa or your bank, and you give us only the reference.

If you contact us

Our contact form does not store your message on our website. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send, so the message, your phone number and the details you include are handled by WhatsApp and received by us there. If you call or message us directly, we receive what you send.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to show your name and picture when you are signed in.
  • To keep your saved stories.
  • To send you news updates by email only if you switch them on. You can switch them off at any time in My Account.
  • To reply to messages, news tips and correction requests.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent misuse.
  • For advertisers: to manage bookings, review adverts, issue invoices and receipts, verify payments, send campaign updates and keep financial records.
  • To count advert views and clicks and detect fake activity, as described above.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use readers' personal data to target adverts.

4. Legal basis

  • Your consent — for creating an account and for email updates. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Providing the service you asked for — for keeping your account and saved stories.
  • Legitimate interests — for keeping the site secure, answering messages you send us, and measuring advert views and clicks without identifying readers.
  • Contract and legal obligations — for advertiser bookings, invoices and payment records, which we must keep for tax and accounting purposes.

5. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the site. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase — stores account data (name, email, picture, saved stories, email-update choice), advertiser records, advert artwork and advert view and click counts. Our database is hosted in London, United Kingdom.
  • Google — handles "Sign in with Google". Google also serves the fonts used on the site.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website.
  • Content networks (jsDelivr and cdnjs) — deliver code libraries the site uses.
  • Data services — the weather and markets panels load live data from OpenWeatherMap / Open-Meteo, currency-api, gold-api and CoinGecko. These receive your IP address but none of your account details.
  • Image hosts (Unsplash and Pexels) — serve some story photos.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to contact us through WhatsApp.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from a Kenyan authority.

6. Data outside Kenya

Some of the providers above store or process data outside Kenya, including in the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United States. Where this happens, we rely on providers that apply recognised data protection safeguards, and we transfer only what is needed to run the site.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data and saved stories: for as long as you have an account. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days.
  • Email-update choice: until you switch it off or delete your account.
  • Messages to us: only as long as needed to deal with them.
  • Advertiser invoices and payment records: for as long as Kenyan tax and accounting law requires.
  • Advert view and click records: up to 24 months, then deleted or kept only as totals.
  • Advertising enquiries: up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become an advertiser.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, contact us. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya at odpc.go.ke.

9. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, your browser stores a small sign-in token in its local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it. For advert counting, your browser keeps a random session code and a short-lived copy of the current adverts in its session storage; both are removed when you close the tab.

10. Children

Our news is for a general audience, but accounts are intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Account data is protected by access rules in our database so that each person can see and change only their own information, only authorised IMMA Media staff can see the email-update list, and each advertiser can see only their own campaigns, invoices and results. No method of storing data online is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our site changes. We will change the "Last updated" date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell signed-in readers.
