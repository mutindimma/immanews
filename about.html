Who we are

Your World. Your Stories. Your Media.

IMMA News is a Kenya-based digital news and information platform dedicated to making important stories accessible, engaging and relevant to everyday life. It is owned and published by IMMA Media, a content, broadcast and production company.

We cover developments across Kenya, Africa and the international community, providing readers with news, practical information and insights that help them stay informed.

Our coverage includes international affairs, sports, health and wellness, travel and practical guides, markets and weather.

Editorial standards

Our Editorial Commitment

  • Accuracy first. We aim to publish accurate, clearly written information and check key facts against the original sources.
  • Credited sources. Every story names the reporting or official sources it is based on, with links where available.
  • Honest images. Photos are licensed for reuse and credited. Illustrative or AI-generated images are labelled as such.
  • Clear labelling. We keep factual reporting separate from opinion and sponsored content, and label each clearly.
  • Prompt corrections. When an error is identified, we review it and correct it promptly. Report an error.
  • Fairness and respect. We value transparency, fairness and respect for our readers and the people we write about.
