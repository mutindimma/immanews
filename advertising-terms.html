Advertising Terms

The rules for buying adverts and sponsored content on IMMA News.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

These terms apply when a business, organisation or person ("you") books advertising or sponsored content on immanews.com ("IMMA News"), which is owned and run by IMMA Media of Nairobi, Kenya ("we", "us"). You accept them when you create an advertiser account or submit a campaign. Our general Terms of Use and Privacy Policy also apply.

1. Booking and approval

  • A booking is a request until we approve it. We review every advert and sponsored article before it appears and may ask for changes or decline it.
  • We check availability when you submit and again when we approve. A small number of adverts rotate in each space. A space is exclusive only if we agree that in writing.
  • An approved campaign runs from its start date to its end date once payment has been confirmed. If payment arrives after the start date, the campaign still ends on the booked end date unless we agree otherwise.
  • Prices are shown in Kenya shillings and are those listed on the site at the time you submit, less any valid discount code.

2. Payment

  • You can pay by M-Pesa or bank transfer using the details shown in your dashboard after approval. Enter your M-Pesa confirmation code or bank reference so we can match the payment.
  • Your advert goes live only after we have confirmed the payment, unless we have agreed in writing that it is complimentary.
  • We issue an invoice when you submit a campaign and a receipt when payment is confirmed.
  • If we decline a campaign that you have already paid for, or cannot run it because a space became unavailable, we will refund what you paid for the part that did not run, or move it to other dates with your agreement.
  • If you cancel after a campaign has started, the part that has already run is not refundable. Contact us about cancelling before the start date.

3. Content we do not accept

Adverts and sponsored content must be legal, decent, honest and truthful, and must comply with Kenyan law, including consumer protection and data protection law. We do not accept:

  • misleading or unproven health or medical claims, including "miracle" cures and weight-loss promises;
  • schemes that promise guaranteed or unrealistic financial returns, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, and unlicensed lending, investment or forex trading;
  • illegal products and services, counterfeit goods, and gambling or betting that is not licensed by the relevant Kenyan regulator;
  • content that is hateful, discriminatory, violent, sexually explicit, or that targets children inappropriately;
  • political or election adverts that do not clearly identify who paid for them;
  • adverts that imitate IMMA News stories, pretend to be news, or impersonate another person or organisation;
  • links to pages that install software without consent, collect data deceptively, or contain malware.

We may remove an advert at any time if it breaks these rules or if we receive a credible complaint, and may suspend an advertiser account that repeatedly does so.

4. Sponsored content

  • Sponsored articles are always labelled Sponsored Content and show who paid for them. They are kept separate from our independent journalism.
  • Our editors may correct facts, spelling and tone, and may decline content that does not meet our standards. Advertisers cannot publish directly on the site.
  • Paying for advertising does not influence our news coverage.

5. Your artwork and rights

  • You confirm that you own or have permission to use everything in your advert, including logos, photos, music and the people shown.
  • You allow IMMA Media to display your advert on IMMA News for the booked period and to show it in your invoices and reports.
  • Files must be JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB. Animated adverts must not flash rapidly or play sound.

6. Reports

Your dashboard shows views and clicks measured by IMMA News. A view is counted when at least half of the advert is on screen for one second. We filter out known bots and repeated views and clicks, but no measurement is perfect, and figures are provided as a guide. We do not guarantee any particular number of views, clicks or sales.

7. Liability

You are responsible for the content of your adverts and the pages they link to. To the extent the law allows, IMMA Media's total liability for a campaign is limited to the amount you paid for it, and we are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under Kenyan law that cannot be limited.

8. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and our prices. Changes do not affect campaigns already approved. Questions: call or WhatsApp +254 785 887 830, or use our Contact page.
